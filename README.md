# Meridian Feedback

Meridian is a focus timer built around a live globe, helping you see where your
time goes as it happens. It runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac and the web.

This repo is where you can report bugs and share ideas. Every issue gets read.

## Submitting feedback

1. Click **Issues**, then **New Issue**
2. Choose the bug report template, or open a blank issue
3. Describe what happened. Screenshots always help, and the app version saves a
   round trip

## Anything to do with your account

For your data, your account, or a purchase, email support@meridiantimer.app or
see [meridiantimer.app/support](https://meridiantimer.app/support). Those reach
the team directly, and they are the right place for anything you would rather
not post in public.

## A note

Your feedback shapes what gets built next. Thank you for trying it.
